# Shop operations improvements — 2026-10-31

Repair job management and backup/restore are excluded from this change.

## Daily workflows

- **Customers:** Sales → Customers provides contact details and credit terms. Open a customer for purchases, payments, return credits, refunds and balance aging. Statements include only accessible locations; credit invoices require credit-view permission. Cash purchases can also link to a saved customer in POS → Customer.
- **Credit terms:** A blank credit limit means no configured limit; zero prohibits new unpaid balances. Default payment terms are 30 days and can be set from 0–365 days. A new credit sale may specify its due date. Adding to an existing invoice keeps its original due date. Overdue debt prevents extending further credit; fully paid purchases and collections remain available. Aggregate limits include debt across branches, while the staff statement only reveals authorized locations. Existing invoices without a due date remain explicitly undated and are not retroactively declared overdue.
- **Customer permissions:** Existing `customers.view` and `customers.create` permissions control browsing and creation. The independent `customers.update` permission controls changes to details, terms and limits. The registered owner has access; owners may grant the new permission through Roles. Existing customized role grants are preserved.
- **Minimum prices:** Final line values after both line and allocated order discounts must meet the configured minimum. Selling below it requires both enabled POS price overrides and the price-override permission.
- **Reorder levels:** Inventory → Reorder levels / alerts manages a threshold for each SKU at each location. A blank threshold disables alerts; zero alerts at zero stock. Available stock includes quantity, saleable IMEI counts and bundle capacity. Inventory view permission allows reading; inventory adjust permission is also required to save a threshold. Dashboard attention cards link to low-stock alerts.
- **Held carts:** Hold cart saves to the database at the selected location. Authorized cashiers at that location can resume it on another counter. Up to 100 held carts are retained per location; a full queue produces an error instead of silently deleting older carts. Resuming atomically removes the held record so another counter cannot resume it twice. Once resumed, the current cart is restored through browser session storage. Use Hold cart again before handing it to another counter. Existing local held carts are imported on POS startup; they are not removed from local storage if upload fails. Sales, stock and price permissions are revalidated at checkout. Held carts do not reserve stock.
- **Tax:** Business settings contains a configurable **tax included in selling prices (%)** rate, default zero. It applies after discounts; payment totals stay unchanged. Tax amounts are stored on posted lines. Partial returns consume the original tax snapshot with rounding reconciliation, and replacements retain the returned tax even if the configured rate changes. No legal tax rate is preselected. Existing posted records retain their original values.
- **Reports:** Sales, damaged-stock, out-of-stock and expenses reports have filtered CSV downloads. Exports include all matching records, preserve location/shift scope and hide damaged cost values without cost-view permission. Spreadsheet formula prefixes in text are escaped. Sales and collections use their respective transaction dates. The period residual is labeled separately from actual invoice debt. Dashboard gross profit excludes included tax and returned margin, but is not business net profit after operating expenses.
- **Dashboard:** Permission-filtered attention cards show overdue invoices, low-stock items by location and cash differences awaiting approval. Repair-related cards are deliberately absent.
- **POS:** Mobile users can switch between products and cart/payment panels. Desktop shortcuts are Alt+S for search, Alt+H to hold and Alt+P to focus payment. The current cart remembers its customer, notes, due date and checkout request key on refresh. Customer search retrieves at most 20 matches per request. Customer selection, discount dialogs and receipt-completion UI are separate components.

## Engineering

Purchasing model casts, date/enum annotations, validation-rule imports, route model typing, inventory argument typing and factory model lookups were corrected without static-analysis suppressions. CI now provisions the MySQL test service used by phpunit.xml.

Apply the included migrations with `php artisan migrate`. They add nullable/defaulted financial metadata, reorder levels, server-held carts and the independent customer-update permission. They do not rewrite historical financial values or add due dates to old invoices. The build generates frontend route helpers.

The final full test suite passed **330 tests with 3,736 assertions**. Project-wide PHPStan passed with zero errors; frontend formatting/lint, TypeScript, production build and PHP formatting passed. The new migrations were applied to the local application database.

Production-built pages were exercised in headless Chromium with synthetic preview records at 1440 px and 390 px widths. Customer directory, statements, reorder controls and POS were checked for horizontal overflow. Discount entry, shared-cart save/resume and server customer search/selection passed. A 390 × 667 px credit dialog was checked for bounded height, scrolling and a reachable completion action. Preview artifacts are under `/tmp/pos-print-check/artifacts/shop-operations`. These browser checks complement the server regression tests; they do not simulate a production rollout or physical printer.
